# TNAU Cotton Seasons and Duration
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_seasonandvarieties_cotton.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: crop calendar, varieties

TNAU lists winter-irrigated cotton sowing in August-September with a 150-160 day duration. Summer-irrigated cotton is listed for February-March, also with a 150-160 day duration. Rice-fallow cotton is listed for February-March with a 125-135 day duration. The source gives district-specific entries and separate rainfed seasons; sowing windows and varieties therefore depend on district and production system.
