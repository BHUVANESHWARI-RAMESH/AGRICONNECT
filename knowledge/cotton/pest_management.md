# TNAU Cotton Fruit Borer Management
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/crop_protection/cotton/crop_prot_crop_insectpest%20_cotton_1.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: pest management

TNAU's cotton fruit-borer page describes circular boreholes in bolls and granular frass outside the holes as damage signs. Its integrated-management section includes monitoring, synchronized sowing, avoiding continuous cotton cropping and ratooning, removing crop residues, and careful water and fertilizer management. The source also cautions against indiscriminate insecticide use. Consult the current source and local agricultural extension guidance for control decisions.
