# TNAU Cotton Fusarium Wilt
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/crop_protection/cotton_diseases/cotton_d1.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: disease management, diagnosis

TNAU identifies cotton Fusarium wilt as caused by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. vasinfectum. The source lists yellowing and browning of cotyledons, drooping or wilting leaves, vascular browning, stunting, and fewer bolls among symptoms. It describes seed and soil as routes by which the pathogen can persist or spread, with wind and irrigation water involved in secondary spread. The source page contains additional management details and should be checked directly before decisions.
