# TNAU Cotton Harvesting
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cotton_harvesting.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: harvesting

TNAU's cotton harvesting page says to harvest kapas at intervals of 5-7 days and shade-dry it immediately after harvest to avoid discolouration. This note reports the source's stated practice without extending it to other crops or harvest systems.
