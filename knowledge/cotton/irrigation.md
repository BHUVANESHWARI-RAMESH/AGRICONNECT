# TNAU Cotton Irrigation Management
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cotton_irri_irrigation%20management.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: cultivation, irrigation

The TNAU cotton page says to irrigate immediately after sowing and provide life irrigation on the fifth day. It describes different irrigation frequencies during vegetative, flowering, and maturity phases, and says to regulate irrigation based on soil type, weather, and rainfall. It identifies square formation and boll maturity as critical crop stages. Refer to the original page for the full schedule and applicable cotton type.
