# TNAU Harvesting of Transplanted Lowland Rice
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropproduction_cereals_rice_tranpudlow_harvesting.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: harvesting

TNAU describes draining field water 7-10 days before the expected harvest for transplanted puddled lowland rice. Its maturity indicators include about 80% of panicles turning straw colour and hard-dough grains at the base of a selected panicle. The page says to harvest, thresh, and winnow when these signs indicate maturity, and to dry grain to 12% moisture for storage. The guidance is for the rice system named by the source.
