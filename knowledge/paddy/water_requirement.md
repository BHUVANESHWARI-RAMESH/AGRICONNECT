# TNAU Paddy Water Requirement Notes
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/expert_system/paddy/cultivationpractices3.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: cultivation, irrigation

The TNAU paddy expert-system page reports an average water requirement of 1,100 mm and an average of 700 mm for System of Rice Intensification (SRI). It says daily consumptive use varies from 6 to 10 mm and that total rice water use ranges from 1,100 to 1,250 mm depending on agro-climatic situation, variety duration, and soil characteristics. Its SRI section describes early irrigation to moisten soil, then irrigation after hairline cracks form, with a later change in water depth after panicle initiation. Values are source-specific and vary with conditions.
