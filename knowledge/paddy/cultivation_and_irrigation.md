# TNAU Paddy Water Management for Transplanted Rice
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_irrigationmgt_cereals_rice1.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: cultivation, irrigation

For transplanted rice, the TNAU irrigation page describes shallow water of 2 cm at transplanting and maintaining that depth through the first seven days after transplanting. After establishment, it describes cycling submergence with 5 cm water depth during the crop period. It identifies panicle initiation, booting, heading, and flowering as critical water-requirement stages. The page also says to provide drainage for excess water and gives a last irrigation timing of 15 days before harvest. These are the practices stated on this source page; conditions and crop systems can differ.
