# TNAU Paddy Bacterial Leaf Blight
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/expert_system/paddy/cpdisblb.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: disease management, diagnosis

The TNAU paddy crop-protection page describes bacterial leaf blight caused by Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae. It lists early seedling wilt (kresek), water-soaked to yellowish stripes on leaf blades, yellow or whitish marginal lesions, leaf drying, and bacterial ooze as symptoms. It describes squeezing the lower end of an affected seedling to distinguish bacterial ooze from stem-borer injury. Use the linked source for its full diagnosis and management details.
