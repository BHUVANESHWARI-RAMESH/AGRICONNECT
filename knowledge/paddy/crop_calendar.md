# TNAU Tamil Nadu Rice Seasons
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropproduction_cereals_rice_season.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: crop calendar, varieties

TNAU's district-and-season table lists, for Thanjavur, Nagapattinam, and Tiruvarur, Kuruvai during June-July, Samba during August, Late Samba or Thaladi during September-October, and Navarai during December-January. The table also associates varieties with district and season. These are Tamil Nadu crop-calendar entries, not a universal calendar for every district or growing system.
