# TNAU Groundnut Leaf Spot and Rust
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/org_farm/orgfarm_prac_agri_groundnut_diseases.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: disease management, diagnosis

TNAU describes early and late leaf spot (tikka) as distinct groundnut diseases. The source notes that early leaf-spot lesions can have a yellow halo, while late leaf spot can produce small dark lesions and premature leaf drop. It also describes rust pustules on the lower leaf surface. The management notes include sanitation, removing diseased plant debris, crop rotation, and avoiding groundnut monocropping.
