# TNAU Groundnut Production Guide
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/CropProduction/Oilseeds/oilseeds_groundnut01.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: cultivation, irrigation, crop calendar, harvesting

TNAU's groundnut guide lists rainfed Chithirai sowing in April-May, early Adipattam in June-July, and late Adipattam in July-August. For irrigated groundnut it lists Margazhi in December-January, Maasi in February-March for the New Delta areas, and Chithirai in April-May. The guide emphasizes adequate moisture during flowering, pegging, and pod development. It describes maturity signs including drying and falling older leaves, yellowing top leaves, and brown-black inner pod shells. It says to irrigate before harvest when soil is dry to ease lifting. Exact crop schedules vary by soil and season.
