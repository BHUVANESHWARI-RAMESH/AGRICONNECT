# TNAU Banana Cultivation Guide
Source URL: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/horticulture/horti_fruits_banana.html
Source Organization: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)
Topics: cultivation, irrigation, crop calendar, pest management, disease management, harvesting

TNAU says planting season depends on variety and land type; its page lists separate windows for wetland, garden-land, hill, and padugai banana. It describes irrigation immediately after planting, life irrigation after four days, and different later intervals for garden land and wetland. The page lists pests including corm weevil, stem weevil, and banana aphid, and diseases including Sigatoka leaf spot, anthracnose, bunchy top, and Panama disease. It reports a 12-15 month crop duration and bunch maturity 100-150 days after flowering, depending on variety and conditions. Refer to the source for its full variety-specific details.
